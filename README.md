# Task 8: Sales Dashboard – Superstore Analysis

## 🎯 Objective
To create a basic interactive dashboard that visualizes sales performance across product categories, regions, and time periods.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Power BI** – for building and visualizing the dashboard
- **Superstore_Sales.csv** – as the primary dataset

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes the following visuals:

1. **Line Chart** – Sales over time, categorized by product category (e.g., Office Supplies, Furniture, Technology)
2. **Bar Chart** – Sales distribution across different U.S. regions (East, West, Central, South)
3. **Donut Chart** – Proportion of sales by category
4. **Map Visualization** – Shows geographic distribution of sales and sub-categories across U.S. states
5. **Slicer** – Allows filtering by region for dynamic analysis

---

## 📈 Key Insights

1. **West Region Leads in Sales and Profit**  
   The West region contributes the highest to both sales (31.58%) and profit (37.86%), indicating strong customer demand and operational efficiency.

2. **Technology is the Most Profitable Category**  
   Technology category leads with approximately $150K in profit, outperforming Office Supplies and Furniture.

3. **Sales Show Seasonal Patterns**  
   Sales in Office Supplies and Technology show clear seasonal spikes, notably around 2016, possibly due to business cycles and fiscal events.

4. **Sales Density is High in Major Economic States**  
   Sales distribution is broad across the U.S., but states like California, Texas, and New York have higher sales density, suggesting regional focus areas.
